A short round, your way

Choose Rare or Popular, Daily or Practice, a topic, and a character. Daily uses seven timed questions; Practice offers an optional timer. All scoring is editor-estimated, not a live survey. This build uses a fictional campus and its own edited question library.

Before you begin

Progress stays in this browser. An unlisted answer can still be valid; retry or skip, and use the feedback draft for a proposed addition. The current game retains its working title Campus Commons while its final presentation is developed.
