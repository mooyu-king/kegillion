Privacy Policy

Updated November 15, 2026. This policy describes the current development edition.

Local game data

The game uses browser storage to retain settings, active progress, and daily results where available. The website also stores your sidebar preference and favorite game IDs on this browser. This is device-local storage, not an account. Clearing site data can erase progress. We do not provide cloud recovery.

Sharing and feedback

Sharing is initiated by you through your device or copied text. Feedback drafts download locally and are not automatically transmitted. Emailing us shares your email address and the information you choose to include with the email provider and recipient.

Analytics and hosting

This development edition does not load analytics or advertising scripts. A planned production analytics service is not active. Before activation, this policy will be updated to describe it. A future hosting provider may process technical request information when the site is published.

Your choices

You can avoid sharing results, omit personal information from feedback, and remove local browser data. Contact mooyuking@gmail.com with privacy questions. Please do not send sensitive personal information.
