About Us

Kegillion is a developing collection of campus-themed browser experiences. The first game explores a fictional American campus with original character artwork and an edited question library.

What we are building

Our aim is to make short quiz sessions understandable: clear mode choices, readable scoring explanations, and useful ways to practise. The website and local game are still being developed. Future regional collections are ideas, not available products.

Editorial approach

Scores estimate familiarity; they are not measured student survey results. The current library is not exhaustive or independently fact-checked in full. We welcome precise corrections and do not present the game as academic assessment.
