Report an answer

Include the exact prompt, the response you entered, and a short explanation of why it fits. A reliable reference is useful for factual corrections. A locally downloaded feedback draft is not submitted until you choose to send it.

Report a technical issue

Describe the page, your browser and device type, the action you tried, and what happened. Do not include passwords or private browser data. For an accessibility issue, tell us which control or passage caused difficulty.

What to expect

Feedback informs development, but we cannot promise a response time, acceptance of every suggestion, or a date for future features.
