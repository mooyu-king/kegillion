# Kegillion

[Visit Kegillion](https://kegillion.games/) · [Explore the Game](https://kegillion.games/kegillion-game/) · [How to Play](https://kegillion.games/how-to-play/)

![Kegillion campus mascot badge](brand/kegillion-logo.webp)

**Campus culture. Curious minds.** Kegillion is a developing browser-based campus trivia collection, starting with a fictional American college setting and a friendly host, Mia.

![The illustrated Kegillion campus](art/campus-quad-v2.webp)

## Find Your Kind of Clever

- **Two scoring directions:** look for less familiar accepted answers in Rare mode, or more familiar answers in Popular mode.
- **Seven-question sessions:** try a timed Daily set or explore Practice with an optional timer.
- **Five topics:** campus life, food and snacks, games and sports, music and screen culture, and schools and the wider world.
- **Your campus character:** choose a student avatar and follow Mia's feedback throughout a round.
- **Favorites:** save games on your current browser and revisit them from [Your Favorites](https://kegillion.games/favorites/).
- **Helpful guides:** read the [scoring and gameplay guide](https://kegillion.games/how-to-play/) or explore [campus culture](https://kegillion.games/campus-culture/).

## A Growing Campus Collection

The US campus game is the current development edition. European, Japanese and Korean, Australian, and South American campus experiences are future ideas, not available games. No release dates are announced.

## Know What the Scores Mean

Scores are editorial estimates, not live student survey results. The podium crowd is an illustration. The answer library is limited and remains under review; an unlisted answer may still be reasonable. Progress and favorites stay in the current browser when storage is available, without an account or cross-device synchronization.

This is a development edition. Artwork, question coverage, and the experience may change. The website address above is the intended public destination; this repository does not establish that hosting is live.
