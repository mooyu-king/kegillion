How to play Kegillion

Choose your direction. Read the prompt. Make your answer count.

1. Set up your round

Choose Rare for less familiar accepted answers or Popular for more familiar ones. Select Daily or Practice, a topic, and one of six cosmetic avatars. Daily uses seven fixed questions per topic and Eastern calendar date; switching modes does not replace the daily set.

2. Answer the actual question

Daily allows 25 seconds per question. Practice can be untimed. Enter one clear response and submit. Unlisted answers remain open for another try; they are not proof that your idea is false. Skip if you cannot find an accepted entry.

3. Understand your score

ResultRarePopular

These scores are edited familiarity estimates. The podium crowd is an illustration. A skip or timeout is a miss: Rare receives a 101 penalty and Popular receives 0. A missed question never earns a crown.

4. Review and practise

Read each answer explanation, then finish the seven-question round. The first daily finish per topic is stored locally; subsequent runs are practice replays. There is no global ranking or cross-device account. Share your result if you want, or copy the text when device sharing is unavailable.
